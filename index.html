Why Venus

A school built on purpose, not just promise.

We're one of the leading secondary schools in Juba. Our mission is simple: to raise a young generation for peace, in a community where every student is a Citizen.

  • Ministry Accredited
  • Small Class Sizes
  • Boarding Available
01 / Academics

Rigorous, joyful learning

Small classes, great teachers, and a curriculum designed to spark curiosity. Our students don't just pass exams. They learn how to think.

  • Small class sizes
  • Experienced subject specialists
  • Personal mentorship for every student
02 / Agriculture

The living laboratory

Our school farm isn't a side project. It's a classroom. Students learn science, business, and hard work by growing real things in real soil.

  • Hands-on agricultural program
  • Science meets practical farming
  • Entrepreneurship through produce
03 / Athletics

Champions in motion

Football, athletics, and track. Our teams compete in every event and bring home the wins. But what we're really coaching is character.

  • Regional athletics champions
  • Football, track, and field
  • Discipline and teamwork above all
04 / Community

Citizens for life

Our students call themselves Citizens. It's not a nickname. It's a promise that they belong here, and that they'll carry Venus with them wherever they go.

  • Students known by name
  • Unity Day every November
  • Alumni who stay connected
